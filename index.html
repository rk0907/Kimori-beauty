Simple Process

How to Restore Your Dark Hair

Two steps. Pure ingredients. Beautiful results.

1

Apply Henna

Mix pure Henna with warm water into a smooth paste. Apply from roots to ends and leave for 2–4 hours. This creates the perfect base and conditions the hair.

2

Follow with Indigo

After rinsing the Henna, apply freshly mixed Indigo. Leave for 1–2 hours. The Indigo reacts with the Henna to create deep brown to black tones.

3

Reveal & Enjoy

Rinse thoroughly. Your gray is covered, your hair feels stronger, and the colour continues to deepen over the next 24–48 hours.
